Side A

On shift

By day (and plenty of nights), Joseph is on the wards, looking after patients. He’s a junior doctor, which means a new rotation, a new department and a new set of colleagues every few months. Before he is a bass player, a Catan tyrant or a long-suffering Spurs fan, he is a doctor.

  • Junior doctor

    In training in the NHS, caring for patients on busy hospital wards.

  • On rotation

    Moving between specialties and hospitals around south London as part of his training.

  • Days & nights

    Shift work means the bass practice happens at very odd hours.

Need medical help?

Joseph can’t give medical advice through this website or social media. Please contact your GP, use NHS 111 online or call 111, and in an emergency call 999.
